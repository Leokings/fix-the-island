# Fix the Island — player and demo guide

Play: https://fix-the-island.vercel.app/#play

## What you are learning

Restore an island by building working repairs and explaining your choices.
The puzzles teach systems thinking: balance, pressure, structural support,
gearing, protected circuits, water sharing and environmental trade-offs.
This is not a SQL tutorial or professional engineering training.

## Start playing

Use a landscape screen. On desktop, move with WASD or arrow keys and press E
near a repair to interact. On touch devices, use the movement control and
Interact button. Follow the next-repair marker; you can explore the island
without completing repairs first. Repair prerequisites restrict jobs, not land.

Open a repair, read its short objective, change parts/settings, and use Test.
The preview is feedback about your current draft, not a blockchain approval.
Hints explain the next action. Save keeps progress in this browser; it does not
create a cloud account or transfer saves between devices. Sound is optional.

## Eight repair jobs

1. Landing Hoist — balance the lift and protect its moving parts.
2. Spring Pump — route useful water without excessive pressure.
3. Reedbed Sluice — share flow while protecting the habitat.
4. Splitbank Bridge — join and brace a supported crossing.
5. Headland Windmill — choose working, guarded transmission gearing.
6. Fern Path Light — complete a protected circuit with suitable placement.
7. Ridge Cistern — filter and distribute water to both destinations.
8. Lookout Beacon — align a protected signal without disturbing inland habitat.

## Practice and connected play are different

Practice works without a wallet and uses local rules. Connected play, when
configured with verified deployments, submits a construction and explanation
to GenLayer. Its AI-mediated contextual judgment is independently checked by
validators; deterministic repair rules are not delegated to an LLM.

Use your own browser wallet and approve only transactions you intend to send.
Never enter a seed phrase or private key into this game. Testnet GEN is required
for network transactions. A built-in browser without a wallet extension can
still run practice mode; connected play needs a wallet-enabled browser.

Review can take time. You may keep walking while workers prepare the site.
Their animation is not a countdown or proof of approval. Only a verified,
finalized successful approval may restore a connected repair. A rejection
requires revision; a timeout means unresolved, not rejected or approved.

## Demonstrating the project honestly

Show movement, one repair's broken state, a meaningful setting change and its
feedback, then the actual restoration result. Label practice demonstrations
as practice. Show an on-chain result only with its real verified receipt.
Deployment addresses and the latest verification limits belong in
`docs/PUBLIC-DEPLOYMENT.md`; do not imply a contract is live from local tests.

This guide is preparation only. No hackathon entry has been submitted on the
user's behalf.
