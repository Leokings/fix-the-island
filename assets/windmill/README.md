## Materials, UVs and textures

One `AtlasUV` layer per mesh. UVs have inset tile margins and use one 4×2 original atlas layout. Shared albedo is sRGB; roughness and tangent-space normal are linear data. All materials are opaque PBR and export with `doubleSided=true`. Linen additionally uses coincident faces with opposite winding; Blender emits a mesh-validity warning for that topology on fresh export. The GLB imports successfully with finite UVs and the documented count. Any topology cleanup is a later integrated revision requiring its own gate. Bronze uses metallic 0.72; other surfaces use metallic 0. Roughness varies by material and texture, roughly 0.48–0.91 before local weathering. Normal strength in the authored scene is 0.5. glTF export maps roughness into its standard metallic/roughness texture channel layout.
